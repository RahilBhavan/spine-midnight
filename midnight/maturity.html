How positions ended, per maturity date

Repaid: the borrower repaid or bought units back. Rolled: BlueFallbackRolling moved the debt to a Morpho Blue market. Liquidated: price liquidation (normal mode). Late: liquidated in post-maturity mode, where the liquidation bonus grows from 0 at maturity to its maximum (4.38% at 86% LLTV) one hour later. The right-hand group counts only positions that still had debt at the maturity time.

Minutes late, post-maturity liquidations

Minutes between maturity and a position's first post-maturity liquidation, all matured dates. Each minute adds about 0.07 percentage points of bonus at 86% LLTV, up to 60 minutes.
